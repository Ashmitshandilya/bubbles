# Bubbles character spec v2 (art direction)

Status: draft for the founder's approval. Nothing here is on the site yet.

## The brief, in the founder's words
"Cuteness and realism together", a doctor set-up, anime-type charm only if it looks good. Liked references: a pearly white horse-like unicorn
with a long flowing pastel mane in soft swirls; a glossy 3D toy-realistic unicorn with a gold spiral horn, rosy cheeks and one big expressive eye;
glitter stars and curls; soft pastel sky with soap bubbles. Rejected: pig-like snout, lizard-like face, humanoid anime girl, chibi blob.

## Character (what must be true in every picture)
- A real unicorn silhouette: slender neck, refined small muzzle, graceful legs, small neat hooves. Horse-like, never a pig or lizard face.
- Pearly white coat with a soft pink blush on the cheeks and muzzle tip; warm light, never grey.
- One big glossy eye with a violet iris, long lashes and a sparkle highlight (the "anime" part, kept subtle).
- Long silky mane and tail in a pink to lilac to mint pastel ombre, flowing in soft swirls with a few glitter stars.
- A slim golden-pearl spiral horn that glows softly.
- Doctor set-up: a small white lab coat or short cape with pink trim, a pink stethoscope, a tiny nurse cap with a pink cross tucked between the ears, a small pink medical-cross charm.
- Soap-bubble and sparkle motif (the brand name), used lightly.

## Never
Diagnosis imagery (no charts of illness), real people's likeness, copied stock art, scary or sexualised styling. Wholesome and friendly. Original design only.

## Deliverables, in order
1. Concept stills (this document drives them) -> founder picks one.
2. Turnaround (front, side, back, 3/4) of the chosen design, plain background, neutral pose: input for image-to-3D.
3. 3D model (.glb) made with an image-to-3D tool or a 3D artist; integrated on the landing page (react-three-fiber is already installed) with idle motion and cursor-follow. Until then: an illustrated hero with gentle motion.
4. Trademark check on the final mark before launch.

## Acceptance (JEv)
Pass only if: not pig-like, not lizard-like, not humanoid, clearly a unicorn, clearly a doctor, cute at 64px and beautiful at full size, original (no copied stock art), and the founder says yes.
